Contact

Email us at hello@gamenightguide.com.

We read every message, but this is a one-person site, so replies can take a few days.

What to send us

  • A correction: a wrong player count, play time, age or price, with a link to the source if you have one.
  • A game suggestion: a game you think belongs in one of our guides, and who it is best for.
  • A topic request: a “best board games for…” question we have not covered yet.

We do not accept paid placements. A game cannot pay its way onto a list.
