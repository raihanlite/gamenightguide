Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 Nov 2026.

Game Night Guide is a small static website. It does not run analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising trackers of its own.

What we collect

Almost nothing. This site:

  • does not ask you to create an account;
  • does not run its own analytics or cookies for tracking;
  • does not sell or share personal information, because it does not collect any.

The Board Game Finder runs entirely in your browser. Your choices are never sent to a server.

Links to other sites

Our guides link to Amazon, Target, Barnes & Noble and other stores. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may set cookies when you visit them. We have no control over that.

Read their policies if it matters to you.

Advertising

If display ads are added later, the ad provider may use cookies to show ads. This policy will be updated before any ad network is turned on.
