Game Night Guide is a small, independent site. It is written for people in the USA who want to play board games but do not know where to start.

Most board game writing assumes you already know the hobby. We assume the opposite. Every guide answers the same four questions first: how many players, how long does it take, what age is it for, and what does it cost?

## What we do, and what we do not do

- **We do** compare games by facts: player count, play time, age range, and BoardGameGeek rating where we have verified it.
- **We do** say when a price has not been verified, instead of guessing one.
- **We do not** review games we have not researched. Our guides are built from published facts, community ratings, and real play data, not from playing every game on the list.
- **We do not** cover video games. This site is about physical tabletop board games only.

## How we make money

This site earns money in two ways: display ads, and affiliate commissions when you buy through a link to Amazon, Target or Barnes &amp; Noble. A commission costs you nothing extra. Read the full [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

## Corrections

Game facts change: new editions, new prices, new player counts. If you spot an error, tell us on the [Contact](/contact/) page and we will check it and fix it.
