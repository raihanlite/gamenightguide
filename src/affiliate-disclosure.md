Last updated: 22 Nov 2026.

Game Night Guide is reader-supported. Some links on this site are affiliate links, mainly to **Amazon Associates, Target and Barnes &amp; Noble**. If you click one of those links and buy something, we may earn a commission.

## What that means for you

- It costs you nothing extra. The price you pay is the store’s price.
- A commission does not change which games we recommend. Games are picked by player count, play time, age fit and community ratings — not by commission.
- We do not accept paid placements. A publisher or store cannot pay to get a game onto a list.

## What we do not know yet

Affiliate programme sign-ups are not complete at the time of writing. Until an account is approved, links go to the store’s public product pages without an affiliate tag. The disclosure on each guide will be updated when that changes.

## Amazon Associates

Game Night Guide is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.
